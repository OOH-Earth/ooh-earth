## Canonical claims

Use only these claims in the first batch:

1. OOH Earth is an open-source, community-funded adbusting platform.
2. It documents outdoor advertising with location, photo, and notes where a
   contributor provides them.
3. It supports community-led creative resistance, research, photography, and
   artistic remixes.
4. Public records and photos have evidence limits: they do not by themselves
   prove ownership, permission, legality, reach, environmental impact, or
   complete coverage.
5. The immediate objective is 5–10 genuine users who sign up and complete a
   useful contribution, not an unsupported traction or investment claim.

Do not claim delivery as traction, or imply replies, meetings, investment,
ownership, legal structure, authorization, or environmental impact without a
separate dated source.
