# Code of Conduct

OOH Earth is a movement to reclaim public space for the people who live in it. That mission sets the standard for how we treat each other.

## The standard

- **This space is for everyone** reclaiming public space — regardless of background, identity, ability, or experience. Newcomers and first-time contributors are welcome here.
- **Be respectful and assume good faith.** Disagree with ideas hard; never attack people.
- **No harassment, discrimination, or intimidation** of any kind, in issues, PRs, reviews, or any project space.
- **Keep the work non-violent and lawful in principle.** OOH Earth documents, maps, and organises. We stand in the tradition of principled, non-violent resistance to visual pollution — not in anything that endangers people.
- **Don't turn the work against the people it serves.** No using this project, its data, or its community to surveil, dox, harass, or exploit anyone.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions, comments, and contributors that violate this code, and may block repeat or serious offenders from project spaces.

To report a concern, contact **hello@outofhell.org**. Reports are handled discreetly.
